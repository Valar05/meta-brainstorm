# Meta Thesis — Capability Should Outlive the Tool

**Status:** founding doctrine / deliberately falsifiable

## Thesis

Creative work is often packaged by industries as if the institution, toolchain, funding model, distribution channel, and medium were one inseparable object.

They are not.

A large portion of creative practice can be decomposed into reusable capabilities. Those capabilities can cross artifacts and sometimes cross media. When a capability is made explicit, testable, portable, and cheap to invoke, the next creative act becomes less dependent on the institution that previously bundled access to it.

This repository studies that decomposition.

## The flywheel

```text
small experiment
-> real artifact
-> observed lesson
-> reusable capability
-> owned/open implementation
-> lower marginal cost
-> more experiments become affordable
```

The flywheel succeeds only if each turn can produce present-tense value. "Reusable someday" is not enough.

Minimum gate:

```text
SHIPPABLE?  USEFUL?
```

Creativity is permitted to be magnificent, ordinary, accidental, private, communal, commercial, or none of those. It is not required to justify itself through scale.

## Bad House theorem

A system behaves like a Bad House when the participant's activity increasingly serves the house rather than the house serving the participant.

Typical mechanisms include:

- recurring permission or access taxes;
- lock-in that makes accumulated skill nonportable;
- monetization incentives that become design authority;
- artificial scarcity around already-learned capability;
- dependency on a vendor for operations that have become mechanically understood;
- systems that optimize capture, retention, or extraction before the purpose that attracted the participant.

The opposite is not "free of cost." A Good House may be difficult, expensive, technical, or strange. Its distinguishing property is that engaging with it can leave the participant with **more agency and durable capability**.

## Deterministic descent

Use the strongest available cognition where it is actually necessary.

Then ask whether the solved portion can descend:

```text
judgment
-> explicit rule
-> algorithm
-> script
-> test
-> data
-> stable primitive
```

Do not keep paying an intelligence layer to rediscover a mechanism that has already become deterministic.

This applies to AI today, but the doctrine is tool-agnostic. AI is one current source of flexible cognition, not the metaphysical center of the project.

## Creative capability commons

The long-term target is not one omnipotent application.

It is a growing collection of inspectable capabilities that can be reused, modified, composed, and taught.

Examples of the kinds of things that may become capabilities:

- a locomotion law rather than one walk clip;
- a timing grammar rather than one animation;
- a scene compiler rather than one video;
- a material model rather than one texture;
- a narrative state machine rather than one branching scene;
- a sound-generation or arrangement primitive rather than one song;
- a layout grammar rather than one image;
- an acceptance test that preserves judgment after the original creator leaves.

The point is not to erase craft. The point is to preserve whatever part of craft has become transmissible without pretending the rest has vanished.

## Human creativity is the scope, not the conquest target

All human creativity is in scope for study.

That does **not** mean:

- every creative act is reducible;
- every medium should be automated;
- everyone should create in the same way;
- human performance is merely training data;
- cultural, relational, tacit, embodied, or historical meaning can be flattened into parameters without loss;
- technical possibility creates an obligation to build.

It means every creative domain is eligible for the same respectful question:

> **What can be made easier while preserving or increasing the creator's custody of the work?**

## Authorship criterion

Implementation labor and design authorship are different variables.

A useful authorship test asks where the artifact's load-bearing causal structure came from:

- representation;
- constraints;
- invariants;
- tradeoffs;
- failure recognition;
- acceptance criteria;
- revision judgment.

Typing volume is not a sufficient proxy.

## Falsifiers and limits

This thesis weakens when any of the following repeatedly occurs:

1. decomposed capability fails to transfer beyond its founding specimen;
2. abstraction removes the qualities creators actually care about;
3. maintenance cost exceeds the future friction saved;
4. the reusable layer creates more lock-in than the tool it replaced;
5. deterministic descent makes revision harder rather than easier;
6. the system centralizes creative authority while claiming to distribute it;
7. people gain output volume while losing meaningful custody of decisions.

Those are not edge cases to hide. They are primary evidence.

## Compact doctrine

> **Build artifacts that leave capability behind.**

> **Use powerful tools to discover laws, then preserve the laws somewhere more durable than the tool.**

> **Reduce friction without reducing authorship.**

> **The goal is not infinite content. The goal is increasing human freedom to make real things.**
